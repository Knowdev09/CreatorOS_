CreatorOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Who we are

CreatorOS is a private desktop application used by its operator and their team members to manage their own YouTube and TikTok channels. Contact: johnstories50@gmail.com.

Data we access

When you connect a channel through Google or TikTok, you grant CreatorOS access to:

  • YouTube (Google): channel name, profile picture and subscriber count; your uploaded videos and their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and statistics; channel analytics (views, likes, comments, subscriber changes); and permission to upload and edit videos on your channel.
  • TikTok: your display name, username, profile picture and follower/like counts; your public videos and their statistics; and permission to publish videos you choose to your account.
  • CreatorOS account: your email address and password (stored hashed by our authentication provider).

How we use it

Data is used only to show you your own channels, videos and statistics, and to upload, schedule or edit videos when you ask the app to. We do not sell data, show ads, use data for advertising, or share it with anyone outside your CreatorOS workspace.

Storage and security

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication). Access tokens from Google and TikTok are encrypted at rest, kept only on the server, and never shown to other users. Video files are sent directly from your computer to YouTube or TikTok and are not stored by CreatorOS.

Google API Services

CreatorOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. CreatorOS uses YouTube API Services; by connecting a YouTube channel you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Disconnecting a channel in CreatorOS (Settings → Channels) revokes the token and deletes its stored data. You can also revoke access at Google Account permissions or in TikTok's settings under "Security → Manage app permissions". To delete your CreatorOS account and all related data, email us; we delete it within 30 days.

Retention

Data is kept while a channel is connected and deleted when it is disconnected or the workspace is deleted.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes.
