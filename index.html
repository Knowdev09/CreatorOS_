CreatorOS

CreatorOS is a private desktop application that a small team uses to manage its own YouTube and TikTok channels from one place.

What it does

  • Shows statistics for the team's connected channels: views, likes, comments, subscribers and followers.
  • Lists the team's own uploaded videos and plays them with the official YouTube and TikTok embedded players.
  • Uploads videos from the user's computer to the user's own connected channels, including scheduled publishing on YouTube.
  • Lets two team members share one workspace.

Channels are connected only by their owners through the official Google and TikTok authorization screens. Access can be revoked at any time.
